# GTM Context

Maintain versioned context for:

- ICP and exclusions
- Services and verified service descriptions
- Approved claims and prohibited claims
- Geographic and role constraints
- Outreach tone and copy limits
- Suppression and compliance requirements
- Campaign-specific hypotheses

Every update must record the source, owner, date, confidence, and approval status. Treat unverified service details as unknown. Do not convert a draft assumption into an approved claim.
