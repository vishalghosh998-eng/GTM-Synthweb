# 02 Qualification

## Purpose
Use inexpensive checks to decide whether a lead deserves deeper research.

## Checks
- Geography
- Company and product relevance
- Relevant engineering role or explicit development signal
- Source URL availability
- Obvious disqualifiers
- Technology overlap when explicitly stated

## Output
- `qualified`: enough evidence for targeted research
- `nurture`: potentially relevant but insufficient current evidence
- `rejected`: clear mismatch or invalid source

## Hard rules
- Do not infer hiring difficulty.
- Do not award points for unknown budget.
- Do not resolve contacts at this stage unless required by the source.
