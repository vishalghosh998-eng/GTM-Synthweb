# Reply Triage

## Purpose
Classify replies and capture outcomes for learning.

## Categories
- positive_interest
- qualified_conversation
- objection
- referral
- not_now
- unsubscribe_or_do_not_contact
- wrong_person
- negative
- automated_or_unclear

## Rules
- Respect opt-outs immediately.
- Do not infer positive intent from an automated response.
- Preserve the original reply and classification rationale.
- Feed outcome data back into campaign reporting without rewriting historical evidence.
