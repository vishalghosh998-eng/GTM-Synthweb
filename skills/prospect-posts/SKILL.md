# Prospect Posts

Use only publicly accessible, policy-compliant sources. Capture the exact URL, publication date when available, author, verbatim short excerpt, and relevance to the campaign.

Do not use a post merely because it exists. It must connect to engineering delivery, product development, staffing model, or an explicitly stated business priority.

Do not infer private beliefs, sensitive traits, dissatisfaction, budget, or buying intent. A post may support a question, not a definitive claim. If the source cannot be verified, mark the evidence unknown and exclude it from copy.
