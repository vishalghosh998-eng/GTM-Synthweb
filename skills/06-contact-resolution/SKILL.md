# 06 Contact Resolution

## Purpose
Identify the most relevant business contact only after account qualification.

## Preferred roles
CEO/founder for small companies, CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Engineering, or relevant engineering leader.

## Procedure
1. Determine the problem owner from the account situation.
2. Use authorized data sources.
3. Verify name, role, company association, and professional relevance.
4. Verify email using an approved provider if permitted.
5. Record confidence and source provenance.

## Hard rules
- Do not guess email addresses.
- Do not use sensitive personal information.
- Do not contact a person solely because they are the most visible employee.
