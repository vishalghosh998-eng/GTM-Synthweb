# Outreach Safety and Compliance

This repository does not provide legal advice. The campaign owner must confirm applicable rules for the sender, recipient, geography, channel, data source, and message type before launch.

Required controls:

- Maintain suppression and do-not-contact lists.
- Do not contact records with missing or invalid consent/permission status when consent is required.
- Preserve source and collection provenance.
- Honor opt-outs promptly.
- Avoid sensitive personal data.
- Validate sender identity and business information.
- Use provider-approved sending practices and domain authentication.
- Require human approval during the pilot.
