# SynthWeb Initial ICP

## Geography

- United States
- United Kingdom

## Primary account profile

- Product-led software company, SaaS business, technology company, or digitally enabled business.
- Evidence of active product development or engineering hiring.
- A technical requirement compatible with verified SynthWeb capabilities.
- A plausible need for additional engineering capacity, custom development, or an MVP.

## Primary personas

- Founder / CEO when the company is small or the founder owns product delivery.
- CTO
- VP Engineering
- Head of Engineering
- Engineering Manager when they influence staffing or delivery.

## Disqualifiers

- No reliable source for the hiring or business signal.
- No identifiable connection to software/product development.
- Technology or service mismatch.
- Contact role has no plausible relevance.
- Data is private, unlawfully obtained, or cannot be used under applicable rules.
- The record requires an unsupported assumption to justify outreach.
