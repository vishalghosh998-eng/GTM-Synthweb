# SynthWeb Outreach Principles

## Core principles

1. The prospect is the subject. Start from their observed situation, not SynthWeb's biography.
2. The list determines the message. Target a defined condition before writing.
3. Problem before person. Use relevant business or delivery conditions, not trivia from a profile.
4. Use situation → insight → inquisition: state the evidence-backed situation, offer one useful interpretation, and ask whether it is accurate.
5. Earn a reply before requesting a meeting.
6. Every email is a hypothesis. Track signal → message → response → outcome.
7. Use one primary signal and one main insight.
8. Keep the first email concise; default maximum is 75 words unless a campaign owner approves otherwise.
9. Never present an inferred problem as a fact.
10. Never use fake familiarity, generic flattery, invented pain, or unsupported claims.

## Message structure

- Situation: one verified fact, ideally the hiring or product signal.
- Insight: a cautious operational implication relevant to the signal.
- Inquisition: a specific question that invites correction or confirmation.

## Campaign rules

- Define the campaign hypothesis before sending.
- Test one meaningful variable at a time.
- Track positive replies, qualified replies, meetings, bounces, opt-outs, and false-personalization errors.
- Do not scale based on opens alone.
- Do not use a job posting's age as proof of hiring failure.
- Route uncertain or high-value accounts to human review.

## Example CTA patterns

- “Is additional engineering capacity relevant while that role is being filled?”
- “Are you considering external engineering support, or is the plan entirely in-house?”
- “Is this role part of a broader product-development expansion?”

These are templates, not facts about any specific prospect.
