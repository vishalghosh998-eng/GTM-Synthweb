# SynthWeb Service Knowledge Base

This file is a working internal configuration. Verify every commercial detail against the current SynthWeb website and internal delivery leadership before enabling live campaigns.

## Primary campaign: Engineering Pods

Potential use case: additional engineering capacity for product companies with an existing product and ongoing roadmap.

Potential technologies to verify against current delivery capability: React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, AWS, GCP, PostgreSQL.

Potential engagement details to verify before use in copy: team composition, minimum duration, onboarding timeline, pricing, availability, and cost comparisons.

## Secondary campaign: MVP Sprint

Potential use case: funded founders with a validated idea and a defined first product scope.

Any duration, price range, scope, or delivery promise must be confirmed internally before use.

## Secondary campaign: Custom Software Development

Potential use case: bespoke applications, business workflows, integrations, analytics, and other custom software needs.

## Service-fit rules

- Do not recommend Engineering Pods solely because a company has one open job.
- Do not recommend MVP Sprint to an account without evidence of a new product or defined scope.
- Do not use pricing, speed, staffing availability, or savings claims unless they are present in `approved-claims.md`.
- If fit is ambiguous, route to human review instead of forcing a service match.
